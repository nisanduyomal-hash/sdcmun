THE BEGINNING

Six years of
building voices.

Every lasting institution begins with an idea — and the courage of students willing to bring it to life.

The Model United Nations journey at Siridhamma College began six years ago, founded by Vignesh and Manjithe Senanayake. What started as a student initiative grew into a platform for young people to engage with global affairs, practise diplomacy and discover the value of informed debate.

Across successive student leaderships, the club continued to develop its identity and carry its purpose forward. Each generation contributed to the foundations on which the next could build.

FEBRUARY 2026

SDIMUN

International Online Conference

In February 2026, the club organised SDIMUN, its first international online conference. It marked an important milestone in the club's journey — extending its reach beyond the school community and creating an opportunity for delegates to connect through diplomacy in a global online setting.

That experience became part of the foundation for what comes next: a renewed ambition, a wider platform and a new chapter through SDCMUN.

THE INAUGURAL CONFERENCE

SDCMUN'27 brings
a new era.

Building on the work of those who came before, SDCMUN'27 represents the next step in the club's evolution. It is an invitation to bring perspectives together, challenge assumptions and explore the power of diplomacy in a world shaped by complex and competing realities.

“Where Worlds Collide, Diplomacy Prevails.”

OUR THEME

Diplomacy Beyond Extinction.

Our theme draws on a world of extraordinary scale, uncertainty and change. It is a reminder that even when worlds collide and challenges seem overwhelming, dialogue can open pathways forward. Through research, negotiation, cooperation and thoughtful debate, delegates are invited to look beyond differences and consider the possibilities of collective action.
