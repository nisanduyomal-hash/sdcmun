THE STORY BEHIND THE CONFERENCE

About SDCMUN'27

Creating space for dialogue, diplomacy and informed action.

OUR PURPOSE

A platform for
future changemakers.

Siridhamma College Model United Nations is a student-led conference designed to introduce participants to the principles and practice of international diplomacy. Through committee sessions, research, public speaking and negotiation, delegates explore complex global challenges from the perspectives of the countries and stakeholders they represent.

SDCMUN'27 aims to provide a respectful, challenging and academically enriching environment in which delegates can develop confidence, critical thinking and collaborative problem-solving skills.

WHAT GUIDES US

01

Dialogue

Listening carefully, questioning constructively and engaging with differing perspectives.

02

Diplomacy

Building consensus through research, negotiation and respectful representation.

03

Leadership

Equipping young people to think critically and contribute responsibly.

OUR THEME

Exploration meets international affairs.

The conference's Jurassic-inspired visual identity represents discovery, scale and the courage to explore unfamiliar territory. The theme is expressed through the design of the conference while keeping its academic and diplomatic purpose at the centre.
