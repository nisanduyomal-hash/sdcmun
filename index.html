THE CONFERENCE

A meeting of minds.
A purpose beyond borders.

Siridhamma College Model United Nations 2027 is a gathering of young people brought together by a shared interest in international affairs, dialogue and the pursuit of practical solutions. Through committee sessions, research, debate and negotiation, delegates will take on the perspectives of nations and institutions, engage with complex global challenges and work collaboratively towards meaningful outcomes.

Built around the belief that informed dialogue can shape a better future, the conference offers delegates an opportunity to strengthen their public speaking, critical thinking, leadership and diplomatic skills while connecting with a wider community of young changemakers.

A MESSAGE FROM THE SECRETARY-GENERAL

Welcome to
SDCMUN'27.

It is my pleasure to welcome you to the inaugural edition of Siridhamma College Model United Nations. This conference is more than an opportunity to debate international affairs; it is a platform for young people to listen with purpose, question with confidence and work together across differences.

As delegates, you will be challenged to look beyond familiar perspectives, understand the complexity of the issues before you and develop solutions through diplomacy and cooperation. I encourage each of you to approach this experience with curiosity, integrity and an open mind. The ideas you bring, the conversations you begin and the connections you build are what will give this conference its meaning.

On behalf of the Secretariat, I look forward to welcoming you to two days of thoughtful discussion, spirited debate and meaningful collaboration.

Nisandu de Silva Secretary-General · SDCMUN'27
